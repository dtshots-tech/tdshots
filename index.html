The Concept

SHOT,
PRINTED,
DELIVERED.

Most photographers wait to be booked. We don't. We're out on the streets, spotting stunning cars parked up — and shooting them the way they deserve to be shot.

No appointment. No studio. Just us, a camera, and a portable printer. Your car gets photographed, printed on the spot, sealed in a protective bag with our card — and left right on your windscreen.

"While other photographers wait to be found — we go out and find the cars."

Who We Are

SMALL &
STARTING OUT.

New to the scene — and yeah, we know turning up at your car with a camera might raise an eyebrow. Sorry to scare you. We just want to be clean about it. We're two lads who love cars and want to capture the world around us — for you and for us.

Founder & Creator

The one behind the idea, the website, and the lens. Dean came up with the concept of finding cars out in the wild and printing the shot on the spot — something done around the world, but with a T&DSHOTS twist. Always looking for the next great car to capture.

Process

HOW IT
WORKS.

01
👀
We Spot Your Car

Out on the streets, car parks, and roads — if your car stands out, we'll find it. No booking needed, no waiting around.

02
📷
We Take the Shot

Multiple angles, best lighting conditions, professionally composed. Unlike typical photographers, we come to your car — not the other way around.
