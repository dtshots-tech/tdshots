About Us

MORE THAN
JUST CARS.

T&DSHOTS started with a simple idea — photograph the cars people love and give something back. We spot a car, shoot it, print it on the spot and leave it on the windscreen.

Beyond street photography we cover automotive meets, events, music videos and any project that needs a creative eye. Based in Liverpool, shooting everywhere.
