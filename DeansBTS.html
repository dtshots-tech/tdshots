The Story

THE TECH
BEHIND IT.

Computers and code have been part of Deano's life for as long as he can remember. Growing up, if there was a project to build or a problem to solve, he was the one figuring it out — not because he had to, but because that's just how his brain works. Making things is what he does.

T&DSHOTS came about when Deano saw what Tom could do with a camera and thought there was something worth building around it. So he built it — the website, the brand, the whole concept. He goes out with Tom on shoots, helps find spots and keeps an eye out for the right cars, but don't expect to see him in the frame.

Alongside T&DSHOTS, Deano runs LetUsTech — a platform with free Python automation tools and browser games — streams on Kick, and makes content on YouTube. Projects are always on the go. Something's always being built.

"I saw what Tom could do. I just made sure the world could see it too."
