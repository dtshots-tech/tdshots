Process

BEHIND
THE SCENES.

Music video production isn't far from what T&DSHOTS does every day — it's about reading a location, finding the light, and capturing a moment that feels real. Here's how Tom approached it.

01
📍
Location Scouting

Finding spots that work on camera — the same instinct used to spot a great car works just as well for finding the perfect street backdrop for a music video.

02
🎬
Shooting

Tom handled the camera work throughout — capturing performance, movement and atmosphere across multiple takes and locations to bring the track to life visually.

03
✂️
The Edit

Raw footage into a finished music video — cutting to the beat, matching the energy of the track, and delivering something GeorgieLVP could be proud of.

The Person

MEET
TOM.

Tom is the co-founder of T&DSHOTS and the kind of person who notices what everyone else walks past. Everyday cars, everyday streets — but always something worth capturing.

The music video for GeorgieLVP is just one example of what Tom brings beyond automotive photography. It's the same passion, the same eye, just pointed in a different direction.

We're small, we're new, and we're just getting started — but projects like this show exactly what T&DSHOTS is capable of when we turn our lens on the world around us.
