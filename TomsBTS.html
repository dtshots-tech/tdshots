02

The Setup

TOM'S GEAR.

Every shot on this page was taken with this kit. No excuses, no shortcuts — just knowing your tools.

📷
Body
Sony A6400

Tom's daily shooter. APS-C sensor, fast autofocus, and compact enough to take anywhere. Whether it's a car park at midnight or a music video shoot on the streets — this is what's in the bag.

Daily Driver
🔭
Lens
24–70mm Zoom

The go-to lens. Wide enough to get the whole car in a tight car park, long enough to compress a shot and make it look cinematic. Versatile, sharp, and always on the camera.

Go-To
🎯
Lens
50mm Prime

When the shot needs to be clean. The 50mm gives a natural field of view with that bit of background separation that makes a car portrait look intentional rather than accidental.

Portrait Pick
🖨️
Printer
Canon Selphy

The bit that makes T&DSHOTS different. A compact dye-sub printer that means photos go straight from camera to windscreen on the spot — no waiting, no sending files, just a print in your hand.
